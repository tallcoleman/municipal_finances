# Questions to Explore

## From Civic Tech Toronto Members

- What are the trends in municipalities’ police budgets as a % of total spend (and per capita)?
- How has revenue from parking/speeding/other motor fines fallen or risen over the years in different places?
- How has spending on interventions to increase safety of vulnerable [road] users trended?
- What's being downloaded from other levels of government and how does that expense compare to transfers allocated for that service provision?
- Some relative sense of $ spent on car infrastructure per driver vs $ on public transit per rider
- Many municipalities have adopted the Administrative Monetary Penalty System (AMPS) so that a parking ticket is resolved at the municipal level without involving a judge. Does the switch show up as a marked difference in parking tickets revenue?
- An analysis of how Toronto’s spending is proportionally different than the other GTA municipalities (eg I'm expecting that Toronto probably spends more per person on transit and social housing and less on hospital capital funding); plus a similar comparison for rural vs urban municipalities.
- Is there a relationship between municipality size and the rates they can get on their debt?
- Top-level overview -- what are the spending categories and how to the municipalities compare across these categories? 
  - Ex. How does the spending of Halton municipalities (Milton, Burlington, Oakville) compare to Mississauga and Toronto?
  - Is it possible to compare the municipal spending on, say, culture/arts between municipalities of similar size?
